4. 6-Step Multi-Source Conflict Resolution Protocol

Deterministic 5-tier regulatory ranking and mathematical triangulation to eliminate hallucinations and resolve discrepancies between GAAP filings and media reports.

Rank sources into Tiers 1 through 5"] subgraph Tiers ["Source Hierarchy Tiers"] T1["Tier 1: SEC Form 10-K (Audited Statutory Filings)"] T2["Tier 2: SEC Form 10-Q (Unaudited Interim Financials)"] T3["Tier 3: Company Earnings Releases & Transcripts"] T4["Tier 4: Tier-1 Financial Media (Bloomberg, Reuters, WSJ)"] T5["Tier 5: Aggregators, Social Media & Speculation"] end Step2 --> T1 Step2 --> T2 Step2 --> T3 Step2 --> T4 Step2 --> T5 Step2 --> Step3["Step 3: Temporal Recency Filtering
Discard stale data; align to identical fiscal quarters"] Step3 --> Step4["Step 4: Quantitative Triangulation
Cross-reference with mathematical recalculation (e.g. Margin = Gross Profit / Revenue)"] Step4 --> Step5{"Discrepancy Resolved?"} Step5 -->|Yes| Resolved["Adopt Primary Verified Value with Full Provenance"] Step5 -->|No| Step6["Step 6: Explicit Non-Consensus Disclosure
Record both figures side-by-side with confidence intervals & explanation"] Resolved --> FinalReport["Incorporated into Final Research Report"] Step6 --> FinalReport

Statutory Primacy

Audited Form 10-K disclosures strictly supersede quarterly 10-Q, earnings calls, or media speculation.

Triangulation

Requires minimum 3-source cross-referencing before adopting disputed numerical values.

Non-Consensus Rule

When discrepancies cannot be proven mathematically, both numbers are disclosed with confidence bounds.
